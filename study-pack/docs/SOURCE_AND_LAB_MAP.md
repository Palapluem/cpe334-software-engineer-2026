## What stays authoritative

Use the original lecture PDF for course definitions, each Lab sheet for its requirements, and the instructor-approved Week 10 handout for exam scope. Week 10's group-work specification is marked as a draft in the repository guide; confirm its status before using it to settle project grading decisions. The study book does not reproduce every slide and does not claim coverage of Weeks 8–9 or unrelated Computer Architecture uploads.
