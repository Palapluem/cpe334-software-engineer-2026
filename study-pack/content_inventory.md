## Deduplication decisions

- Definitions and methods live in the lecture chapters. Review pages recall them; they do not repeat full worked answers.
- DormFix WBS appears as a tree in Chapter 6. The Gantt appears once in the visual atlas. The casebook points back to them and focuses on the missing RACI evidence.
- The 25-page source-slide screenshot appendix is not part of the reading PDF. A small number of source crops remain only for the Kaching SDS example; the rest of the visual set uses editable vector drawings.
- Exam question/solution repetition is intentional: students receive a clean paper, then a distinct answer section.
